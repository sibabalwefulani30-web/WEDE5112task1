Our Services

The Community Book Nook provides different programs and opportunities for people in our community.

Story Time

Story Time is a program for children and families. It encourages young children to develop a love of reading through stories and activities.

Teen Writers' Workshop

The Teen Writers' Workshop provides young people with an opportunity to develop their writing skills and share their creative ideas.

Adult Book Club

The Adult Book Club gives adult readers an opportunity to read books, discuss stories and connect with other members of the community.

Volunteer

Community members can volunteer their time to support the Book Nook and its programs.

Volunteers can help with community activities, library programs and other activities.

Donate Books

Community members can donate books to help expand the collection available to readers.

Financial Contributions

Financial contributions can help support the Book Nook's programs and activities.
