About The Community Book Nook

Our History

The Community Book Nook began in 2018 as a small pop-up library in a local community centre.

It was founded by retired teacher Eleanor Vance. The library started with only a few shelves of donated books.

As its popularity grew, the Book Nook became a gathering place for local book clubs, children's story hours and community events.

In 2021, the Book Nook moved into a larger permanent space to accommodate its growing collection and programs.

Our Mission

Our mission is to foster a lifelong love of reading and learning by providing free and equitable access to books and educational resources for everyone in our community.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be a vibrant and inclusive hub where the community connects, learns and grows through the power of stories.

Our Target Audience

The Community Book Nook welcomes:

  • Families with young children
  • Local students
  • Avid readers of all ages
  • Community members

Our Founder

Eleanor Vance is the founder of The Community Book Nook. She started the library with the goal of creating a place where people could access books and learn together.
